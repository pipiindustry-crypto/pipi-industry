Seen and held before it carries our name.

We're based in Bali, close to the people who make our goods. Before a piece joins the line, we visit the workshop and hold the samples in our own hands.

  1. Visit the workshop

    We see how each piece is made and who makes it.

  2. Choose the finish

    Shell, metal and fibre, picked with the maker.

  3. Start small

    A few pieces of each, stocked in the US.

  4. Check it by hand

    Only samples that pass our own look go live.
